Our Belief About Early Childhood

The early years shape everything that follows — language, confidence, emotional regulation, curiosity, and independence. Childhood is not a waiting room for the future. It is the foundation of it.

Keizer Rugrats Playschool was designed with intention: a warm environment, steady routines, clear expectations, and meaningful play that supports real growth without rushing childhood.

Families are not just paying for supervision. They are investing in consistency, curriculum-guided learning, a thoughtfully prepared space, daily rhythm, meals, communication, and a provider who knows each child closely.

What Makes Keizer Rugrats Different

This is more than a safe place to spend the day. It is a prepared environment where care, learning, routines, and relationships all work together.

Small and consistent

Children are known closely, routines stay steady, and the group does not feel overwhelming.

Curriculum-guided

Play is supported with intentional learning goals, not random activities pulled together at the last minute.

Intentional materials (not a toy overload)

Every item has a purpose. We avoid clutter and rotating “toy piles” so children can focus, engage longer, and build real skills through play.

Calm environment, not quiet children

Kids can be loud, joyful, and fully themselves. The space is designed with calm colors, clear layout, and predictable routines so energy stays positive and regulated.

Full-day value

Care includes long operating hours, meals, rest time, outdoor play, communication, and a thoughtfully prepared space.

Our Curriculum Framework

A balanced framework that supports both school readiness and childhood — across developmental domains.

Language & Communication

Conversation, vocabulary, stories, expressive confidence.

Early Math Foundations

Counting, patterns, spatial awareness, sorting, and logical thinking.

Self-care, routines, responsibility, confidence, and everyday life skills.

FAQ

Is this a daycare?

Keizer Rugrats Playschool is a Certified Family Child Care program with a playschool feel: small, warm, structured, and curriculum-guided. Children are cared for in a dedicated space with intentional routines, purposeful play, and early learning woven into the day.

What ages do you serve?

We serve children ages 12 months–9 years in a mixed-age setting.

Do you use screens?

Screens are used rarely and only for a clear purpose, such as a safety lesson or guided movement activity. Screens are never used as background entertainment or supervision.

What does a typical day include?

Our day includes arrival routines, breakfast, circle time, creative centers, outdoor or gross motor play, lunch, rest or quiet time, snack, and afternoon play. The rhythm is structured but flexible enough to meet children’s needs.

What if my child doesn’t nap?

Children participate in a calm rest period. Children who do not fall asleep after a reasonable rest window may engage in quiet activities while remaining respectful of resting children.

How does enrollment work?

Families start by submitting the interest form. After that, we talk through your child’s age, schedule needs, routines, and whether the program feels like the right fit. Enrollment is completed through required paperwork and Brightwheel.
